## 0. Is retraining scientifically necessary?

Written after the ablation (`docs/RAPPORT_ABLATION.md`).

**What the ablation settles without retraining:**
- The service guarantee, added at inference, removed the 6 observed clearance failures.
- It never intervened during the demand on these traffics, so it neither cost nor gained anything there.
- The two protections did not interact (D interaction = 0 in 150/150 blocks).
- **Retraining arms "guarantee only" and "minimum green only" would therefore answer no open question here.**

**What it cannot settle:** the minimum green, imposed on policies that rely on 2 s greens:
- raises J by +1.2 to +2.3 s for QMIX and VDN (100 % of pairs);
- leaves every learned run that cleared above the constrained fixed plan on J and on time loss (0 of the 299
  cleared runs below it; the 300th run failed to clear and has no J).

This is a distribution shift. It does not show what a policy **trained** under the constraint can do. The
single-seed pilots trained with both protections give J between 4.32 and 5.48 s. That is close to the
inference-time masked values (4.74–5.97 s) and well above the constrained fixed plan (3.16 s). One seed per
method, however, cannot support a conclusion.

**Answer:**
- A retraining campaign is **necessary only for the claim that matters most for a paper**: whether learned
  controllers *trained* under the common 7 s minimum green reach an optimised fixed plan under the same
  constraint (H3), and whether training with the protections changes service and efficiency (H1).
- It should be limited to the arm `both` (30 trainings), not the 2 × 2 design (60).
- If the research decision is instead to present the work as an evaluation-methodology and negative-result
  study, the pilots can be reported as indicative only, and no retraining is needed. **That decision belongs
  to the researcher.**
- **In both cases, the robustness validation (`PLAN_ROBUSTESSE.md`) should come first.** It is cheap (about
  2.5 h) and tests what the ablation could not: the guarantee during the demand.

## 1. Questions and hypotheses (stated before any result of this campaign)

| ID | Question | Comparison | Decisive indicator |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Does training **with** the protections (minimum green 7 s + guarantee 120 s) change service and efficiency, compared with adding them at inference to policies trained without them? | per method: trained `both` vs trained `none` evaluated under `both` (same seeds 101–110, paired) | primary: D̄ (always defined) and the clearance rate; secondary: J on pairs that both clear |

**No directional prediction is made.** The three questions are answered with the rules of § 6.

## 5. Failure handling

- A clearance failure is a result: never rerun, never imputed.
- J is undefined for it, and it is counted.
- **Primary indicators are always defined:**
  - the clearance rate;
  - D̄ = waiting burden during [0, 3,600) / N.
- J is reported on the pairs that both clear, with their number.
- A technical incident (process crash, no residual state) gives one rerun of the same cell, recorded.

## 6. Statistical analysis (written before the results)

**Unit of replication:** the training seed (10 per method and arm). Traffics are a crossed factor.

**For each contrast (H1 per method, H2, H3):**
1. Per training seed s and traffic k, compute the paired difference d_sk on D̄ (and on J when both runs clear).
2. Per seed, average over traffics: d̄_s.
3. Report the mean and median of d̄_s, the 10 values, and the sign count.
4. Give a 95 % confidence interval by a **two-level bootstrap**: resample the seeds, then the traffics within each seed (10,000 replicates, seed fixed in the script).
5. Clearance rates are compared per seed (paired); failures are listed.

**Multiplicity:** Holm correction over the three methods for H1 (the primary contrast); the other comparisons are descriptive.

**Interpretation rules:**
- "Training with the protections reduces D̄ for method X" only if the corrected interval excludes 0 **and** at least 8 of the 10 seeds agree in sign.
- No statement of equivalence without a pre-specified margin.
- **No claim of superiority over the fixed plans unless the H3 intervals are below 0 on J and D̄ simultaneously.**
